# Standardized 8-Question Scientific Paper Audit Protocol

1. **Core Scientific Problem:** What exact phenomenon or explanatory gap does the paper address?
2. **Hypothesis:** What falsifiable claim or theoretical proposition do the authors formulate?
3. **Mathematical / Computational Model:** What formal equations, rules, or state-space dynamics define the model?
4. **Explicit & Implicit Assumptions:** What boundary conditions, stationarity, or homogeneity assumptions are required?
5. **Key Empirical / Computational Result:** What central figure, phase transition, or metric supports the thesis?
6. **Reproducibility Target:** Which specific experiment or curve is reproduced in our workshop notebook?
7. **Limitations & Failure Modes:** Where does the model break down, and what does it *not* prove?
8. **Open Research Question:** What testable follow-up hypothesis emerges from these limitations?
